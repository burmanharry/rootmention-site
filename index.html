What it is

RootMention is a founder-run SEO practice for product brands, ecommerce stores, SaaS, gyms and local businesses that already have a website. The work is purchase-intent SEO for Google and AI answers: find the questions that can lead to a sale, then build and improve the pages a buyer, or an AI engine, should land on.

It is built for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Grok, not just blue links. The founder runs the same system on his own brands and takes on a limited number of clients at a time. US businesses with an existing website are the intended fit.

What the monthly plan includes

The founding plan is $299 a month, locked while you stay subscribed, for one brand and one website:

  • AI-search content every month
  • On-page and schema fixes
  • AI visibility tracking across ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
  • A monthly progress report
  • A quality check on every piece of content

The first month starts from your audit findings. You approve changes before they go live. You can pause for 1 to 3 months from your board and keep your price. The site states that the price for new sign-ups is set to rise to $399 as spots fill; if you cancel and return later, you rejoin at the price at that time.

Not included

  • Website rebuilds, redesigns and migrations
  • Paid ads and ad management
  • Unlimited technical development

Free audit

RootMention checks your homepage and up to two key pages, then gives you up to three priorities. The audit also looks at buyer questions in AI search and includes a 30-day plan. No payment needed.
