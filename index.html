What it is

RootMention is a founder-run AI SEO practice for supplement and food brands with an existing website, whether you sell on Shopify, Amazon or both. The work is purchase-intent SEO for Google and AI answers: find the questions that can lead to a sale, then build and improve the pages a buyer, or an AI engine, should land on.

It is built for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Grok, not just blue links. The founder runs the same system on his own brands and takes on a limited number of clients at a time.

What the monthly plan includes

One flat plan at $999 a month for one brand and one website:

  • 4 buyer-intent AI-search content pages a month
  • 8 AI-ready FAQ answers a month, with sources
  • Up to 10 on-page and schema fixes a month
  • AI visibility tracking on 25 buyer prompts
  • 10 outreach pitches a month (placements not guaranteed)
  • A monthly progress report on your task board
  • A quality check on every piece of content

The first month starts from your audit findings. Month to month, cancel anytime, no setup fee.

Not included

  • Paid ads and ad management
